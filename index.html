Privacy Policy & Support

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Support

If you need help, want to report a problem, or have a privacy question, contact:

Email: dt-app-support@proton.me

When reporting an issue, please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Please do not send private or copyrighted PDF files unless they are necessary to investigate the issue and you have permission to share them.

Privacy at a glance

Sheet Music Easy Reader does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking. The app does not collect or sell personal information.

Files and annotations

The app accesses PDF files only when you choose them through Apple’s document picker. It uses those files to display sheet music and, when requested, save your annotations.

Your PDFs and annotations remain in the storage location you selected. This may be local storage on your device, iCloud Drive, or another file provider. If you use iCloud Drive or another provider, that provider processes and synchronizes the files under its own terms and privacy policy. Sheet Music Easy Reader does not operate a separate server that receives your documents.

Information stored on your device

The app stores preferences locally, such as onboarding status, autosave and screen-awake settings, annotation appearance choices, and the last viewed page for a document. These settings support app functionality and are not used for advertising or profiling.

Data sharing and retention

The app does not sell personal information or share it with advertisers or data brokers. Documents and app preferences remain until you modify or delete the relevant files, remove the app, or clear its data through your device or storage provider.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Because no account or personal-data collection is required, the app does not ask users to provide their age.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is published.
